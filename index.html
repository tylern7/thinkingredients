Aquafaba PowderTexture

Say goodbye to egg whites with aquafaba powder! Made from chickpea cooking water, aquafaba powder is the ideal egg white replacement. It is carefully concentrated into a versatile powder and is functional across various applications.

The powdered format of aquafaba makes it shelf-stable, easy to handle, and consistent in performance. Whether used to create stable, airy foams for meringues, mousses, and cocktails, providing binding and structural support for baked foods, or to create smooth, stable emulsions in sauces and dressings, aquafaba powder is the perfect clean-label addition to foods!

Carrot FibreTexture · Fibres & Proteins

Carrot fibre is a clean-label dietary fibre derived from carrot pulp, a by-product of carrot juice production, offering texture improvement, moisture retention, and enhanced volume across a range of food applications. Its rapid swelling capacity and strong water-binding properties work without requiring high shear, helping to improve product yield and stability while increasing fibre content without impacting flavour or appearance.

With excellent process stability, carrot fibre is a versatile bulking agent that can help replace traditional thickeners and emulsifiers. Labelled simply as carrot fibre, it supports simpler ingredient lists while delivering valuable functional benefits.

Instant swelling capacity

Bulking agent

Citrus FibreTexture · Fibres & Proteins

Citrus fibre is an ideal clean-label solution for texture improvement in various food applications. Deriving from citrus peels, this natural dietary fibre is a versatile ingredient that can do it all, from retaining moisture in baked goods to emulsifying sauces.

Due to its high water-holding capacity, adding citrus fibre to formulations improves texture, increases water content, and enhances emulsification properties. Listed simply as “citrus fibre”, it performs well as a replacement for modified starches, gums, and other stabilizers, and offers effective gelling at room temperature without causing excessive stickiness. It is especially useful in reducing or replacing common functional ingredients, such as fats or eggs, and can naturally extend shelf life.

Cultured Wheat FlourPreservation · Natural propionate replacer

Cultured Wheat Flour is a natural, clean-label ingredient made through the fermentation of wheat flour, producing naturally occurring propionic acid. This propionic acid effectively inhibits the growth of mold and spoilage bacteria, making it a fantastic alternative to synthetic preservatives like calcium propionate. For manufacturers seeking to maintain product freshness while meeting clean-label standards, cultured wheat flour is an ideal choice.

Cultured wheat doesn't interfere with the yeast in your recipe and can be used in a variety of baked goods, with minimal impact on taste and appearance. Easy to incorporate and simply labelled as “cultured wheat flour”, this natural preservative helps maintain product quality while delivering clean-label products that consumers can trust.

Looking for a Gluten Free Option? Ask us about cultured rice flour - it offers all the benefits of cultured wheat flour in a gluten-free format.

Preservation

Extends shelf life by effectively inhibiting yeast, mold, and bacteria growth.

Minimal Impact

Maintains product flavour, colour, and appearance, and won't kill yeast.

Clean Label

Listed simply as “Cultured Wheat Starch”, making it appealing to label-conscious consumers.

Natural Origin

Made through a natural fermentation process.

Dried VinegarPreservation

Dried vinegar is the ideal ingredient for pH control and shelf life extension. A clean label substitute for liquid and buffered vinegars, it delivers strong acidifying power around pH 4.4 without adding extra moisture to your product. On ingredient lists, it can simply be labeled as “vinegar,” making it an excellent clean label option for a variety of applications.

It performs well in spice blends for meats, as well as in sauces and bakery products. When combined with other clean label preservatives, dried vinegar enhances overall preservative function, helping to extend shelf life and providing broad protection against bacteria, yeasts, and moulds.

Italian Sourdough ConcentratesTaste

Crafted from heritage Italian grains, Italian sourdough concentrates are dried, easy-to-use powdered blends that deliver the authentic flavours and aromas of Italy's baking traditions. Used at just 5-10% of the total flour weight, they eliminate the need for long fermentation times while producing artisanal breads with beautifully crunchy crusts, soft interiors, and unique flavour profiles. Formulated with dehydrated Italian sourdough, malts, bread improvers, and enzymes, dried sourdough concentrates are a great option for simplifying production and eliminating the need for a wet starter. During fermentation, yeast activity is optimized, leading to a great rise and texture.

All sourdoughs are unique! The region of origin, the specific flours used in fermentation, and the natural bacteria and yeast involved all combine to create distinct flavours, colours, and aromas. These concentrates offer producers the flexibility to create unique, authentic, and high-quality sourdough products.

Consistency

Produces a consistent sourdough flavour across batches, ideal for large-scale manufacturers.

Authentic Italian Flavour

Production Efficiency

Eliminates the need for a wet starter and reduces fermentation time.

Diverse

A range of concentrates available to create a variety of sourdough style products.

Modified CelluloseTexture

Modified cellulose offers unique stabilizing and gelling functionality. Used as a thickening agent and texture improver, it can repeatedly form a thermally reversible gel when heated and returns to its original viscosity at room temperature. Modified cellulose is extremely versatile and easy to incorporate, making it an ideal stabilizer for various food applications.

  • Prevents blowout and stabilizes product integrity at high temperatures
  • Creates a strong, juicy bite and improves mouthfeel while maintaining product shape
  • Thickens liquids at low concentrations and stabilizes water-oil emulsions
  • Mimics properties of gluten, offering improved texture, volume, and moisture retention

Natural dough relaxer (GSH) is a clean-label ingredient that improves machinability, reduces spring-back, and enhances softness. Inactive dry yeast is a powdered form of yeast that has been deactivated through a drying process. Natural dough relaxer is versatile across various products and maintains consistent quality while improving texture in baked goods. Simply labelled as “yeast”, this natural and clean-label product enhances product quality without discouraging consumers. Resistant Wheat StarchFibres & Proteins

Resistant wheat starch is a versatile fibre ingredient that enhances the nutritional profile of food products while preserving their taste and texture. As a specialized form of wheat starch, it resists digestion in the small intestine and is instead fermented by beneficial gut bacteria in the large intestine as a dietary fibre.

With a neutral flavour, a bright white colour, and a dietary fibre content of over 90%, it is ideal for increasing dietary fibre and reducing digestible carbohydrates without affecting the flavour or appearance of finished products. Its low viscosity and smooth, non-gritty mouthfeel allow resistant wheat starch to blend easily into formulations.

Specialized wheat dietary fibre

Bypasses digestion in the small intestine and ferments in the colon

Minimal sensory impact

No discoloration in final products and a neutral flavour

Smooth and non-gritty texture

Low viscosity and minimal mouthfeel impacts on finished products

Easy incorporation

Does not disrupt the absorption of formulations and is compatible with wheat starch

Our story

Simple is smart.

Back in 2016, we couldn’t help but notice a problem in the food industry – there were all kinds of interesting, new, and functional ingredients being produced around the world, yet they didn't appear on food labels in North America.

Read our story

At the same time, there was a desire for ‘clean-label’ ingredients - consumers wanted to be able to understand (and pronounce) the ingredients in their food. The industry still relied on old, chemical additives that performed key functions in food. Something needed to change.

And so, Think Ingredients was born with one goal: to help clean up labels without compromising on quality, shelf life, nutrition or taste. We believe less is more and strive to keep ingredient lists simple. We understand ingredients and show our customers exactly how to use them.

In our quest for knowledge in ingredient applications, our latest venture was created. In 2021, Think New Foods, our sister company in Burlington, Ontario, was founded to be the manufacturing arm of our ingredient know-how. With everything we had learned in the formulation of great food products, it just made sense to, literally, put it all together – from baking mixes, functional ingredient blends, and standardized clean-label preservatives. Our SQF-certified dry blending facility combines ingredients together to allow our industrial food customers to focus on what they do best – turning ingredients into delicious, high quality foods.

It's our goal to continue to challenge the way things are done and keep integrating along the ingredient supply chain so we can reliably bring the latest innovations to our customers.

Our focus on science and innovation allows us to keep our customers informed about the latest ingredient technologies and, more importantly how the science translates into foods that are label-friendly, nutritious, safe and taste great.
